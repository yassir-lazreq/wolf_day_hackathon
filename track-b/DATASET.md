# Dataset

The dataset is not in this kit. It is a separate download and it appears on the briefing page when it is released. Re-check the page if the link is not there yet.

What you get: about 1,000 anonymized service and damage cases. Each case has car information plus free-text notes written in German by service staff.

Two things to plan for:

The notes are unstructured. They were typed by people doing a job rather than filling in a schema, so spelling, abbreviations and level of detail vary from case to case. Extracting structure from them is the challenge itself, so budget time for it.

The notes stay in German. Names, phone numbers and addresses are fake, but the text itself is real. Translating it is not part of the task and mostly costs you information.
